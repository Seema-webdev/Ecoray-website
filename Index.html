Frequently Asked Questions

What is an energy saver bulb?

An energy saver bulb is designed to provide useful illumination with lower electricity consumption than traditional incandescent lighting.

Where i can use EcoRay bulbs?

Depending on the model, EcoRay bulbs can be used in bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, offices, shops and other suitable indoor fixtures.

Are EcoRay bulbs easy to install?

Most compatible screw-base bulbs can be installed like a standard bulb. Always switch off electrical power and follow the product instructions.
